### Core Principles

1. **Silence & Hex vs. Mobility**: High-mobility heroes (Storm Spirit, Anti-Mage) are best counteracted with instant disables (**Orchid Malevolence**, **Scythe of Vyse**) before they can use escape abilities.
2. **Illusion Clears**: Illusion-based heroes suffer against **flat damage block** (Crimson Guard) and **unit-scaling AoE abilities** (Echo Slam, Cleave).
3. **BKB-Piercing Utility**: Hyper-carries relying on temporary buffs (Sven's *God's Strength*, Ursa's *Enrage*) are neutralized by support heroes featuring BKB-piercing repositioning or disables (Shadow Demon, Bane).
